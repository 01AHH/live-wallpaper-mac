Installing LiveWall
===================

1. Drag LiveWall into the Applications folder (the shortcut next to it).

2. Open LiveWall from Applications. macOS will say it "can't verify" the
   app — LiveWall isn't signed with a paid Apple developer certificate.
   Click "Done" (not "Move to Trash"), then:
     System Settings → Privacy & Security → scroll down →
     "LiveWall was blocked…" → Open Anyway → enter your password.
   You only need to do this once.

3. LiveWall lives in your menu bar (top right). Its window opens on first
   launch — choose "Online Gallery" in the sidebar to get free wallpapers,
   or use "Add to LiveWall" on the website.

Requires macOS 26 (Tahoe) or later on an Apple silicon Mac.

Full setup guide, optional features and troubleshooting:
https://live-wallpaper-mac-mauve.vercel.app/setup.html
