Get wallpapers into LiveWall

  1. Pick wallpapers here — tick the circle on any tile, or open one.
  2. Add to LiveWall. Your browser asks to open LiveWall; allow it, and the wallpapers download straight into your library.
  3. Or browse the same gallery inside the app: Online Gallery in the sidebar.

Prefer the file? Each wallpaper also has a plain 4K download. Building something else? The whole gallery is available as catalog.json.
